Google's Gemini 3.7 Flash targets coding and agents with 50% introductory price cut

Daily AI News Scan — Saturday, August 15, 2026

Executive Summary

Google accelerated its model release cadence with Gemini 3.7 Flash, dropping API prices 50% just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, as competition from OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and DeepSeek's V4-Pro intensifies. Google also launched Sheets Canvas, a Gemini-powered no-code mini-app builder embedded directly in spreadsheets. Meanwhile, OpenAI expanded ChatGPT's platform reach with a Computer History feature for Mac users and a new Linux desktop client, signaling continued pushes into native OS integration.

Top Stories

1. Google's Gemini 3.7 Flash targets coding and agents with 50% introductory price cut

Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, with the updated model targeting improved performance in coding and agentic workflows. API prices are temporarily halved as Google competes for developer adoption against OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol — now previewing an Ultrafast mode capable of 750 tokens per second — and DeepSeek's newly shipped V4-Pro with adjustable reasoning levels and off-peak pricing. Ramp's August 2026 AI Index offers a cost-use contrast: Anthropic's Fable 5 holds only 6% of enterprise token volume despite double the per-token cost of GPT-5.6 Sol, suggesting price sensitivity is shaping enterprise model selection. A separate security finding underscores agentic risk: encrypted reasoning blocks from frontier models can be reused across sessions and models, with researchers extracting 62 API keys and 33 passwords from user logs.

Source: Google News: Frontier AI

2. Google Launches Sheets Canvas for Gemini Mini-Apps

Google Sheets Canvas is a new Gemini-powered feature that converts spreadsheet data into custom, interactive mini-apps inside Google Sheets without requiring formulas or programming. The launch is part of a broader set of Gemini integrations, including new features on the Pixel 11 device line such as sign-language transcription and improved natural voice input. The move positions Google Workspace as an AI application platform rather than a traditional productivity suite, lowering the barrier for non-technical users to build functional data tools. It also extends Gemini's surface area beyond standalone chat interfaces into embedded, workflow-native contexts.

Source: The Verge AI

3. OpenAI Launches Computer History Feature for ChatGPT

OpenAI launched Computer History, an opt-in feature that enables ChatGPT on Mac to recall user activity across apps and websites without continuous screenshot capture, offering persistent cross-session context. Separately, OpenAI released a ChatGPT desktop application for Linux, supporting Ubuntu 24.04/26.04 LTS, Debian 13, and Fedora 43/44 on both x64 and ARM64 architectures. The two announcements together reflect a strategy of deepening OS-level integration and expanding the addressable user base to developer-heavy Linux environments. The Computer History feature in particular raises ongoing questions about data privacy and the boundaries of ambient AI observation.

Source: Google News: ChatGPT
